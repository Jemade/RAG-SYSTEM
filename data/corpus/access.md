# Access policy — current, 2026-09-01
The fictional Northstar operations platform uses role-based access control. Production access requires hardware MFA and approval from the service owner. Contractors receive read-only access unless a service owner grants a time-limited write exception. Access reviews happen every 30 days. Emergency access expires after 60 minutes. The security contact is security@northstar.example. Shared administrator accounts are prohibited. Access removal must complete within 4 hours of termination. The identity provider is CedarID. Audit records are retained for 365 days.
